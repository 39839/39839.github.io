How to use Text2Anki

      
From raw notes to study-ready Anki cards in under a minute.

    


    
    

      
What is Text2Anki?

      
Text2Anki converts plain text into Anki flashcard decks (.apkg files) that you can import directly into Anki on any device. You choose the card style, the deck name, and the format — the site handles the rest.

    


    
    

      
Card types

      

        

          
Basic

          
Classic question / answer cards. One side shows the question, the other reveals the answer.

          

            Format
            Q: What is ATP?
A: The cell's energy currency.
          

        

        

          
Cloze+

          
Fill-in-the-blank. The hidden word is shown in a highlight when you reveal the card.

          

            Format
            The {{c1::mitochondria}} produce ATP.
          

        

        

          
Cloze++

          
Cloze with an optional hint and a full explanation shown after reveal.

          

            Format
            {{c1::ATP::hint}} powers the cell || Adenosine triphosphate — the energy carrier.
          

        

      

    


    
    

      
Step-by-step guide

      

        

          
1

          

            
Sign in with Google

            
Your decks, card styles, and classes are saved to your account so they're there every time you come back.

          

        

        

          
2

          

            
Choose a Class & Deck name

            
A Class is a top-level subject (e.g. Biochemistry). A Deck is a subtopic within it (e.g. Amino Acids). In Anki this becomes Biochemistry::Amino Acids — a nested subdeck. If you don't need a class, just enter a deck name.

          

        

        

          
3

          

            
Pick a card type

            
Choose Basic for Q&A, Cloze+ for fill-in-the-blank, or Cloze++ for cloze with a detailed explanation. You can mix types across different decks.

          

        

        

          
4

          

            
Paste your notes

            
Paste formatted text into the notes box. The format is detected automatically. You'll see a live preview of the cards that will be generated.

          

        

        

          
5

          

            
Download the .apkg file

            
Click Download .apkg. Open Anki → File → Import → select the file. Your cards appear instantly under the deck name you chose.

          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        
        AI Workflow
      

      
Using AI to format your notes

      
Don't want to format your notes manually? Use the built-in AI prompt generator.

      

        

          

            
          

          
1. Click "Generate AI Formatting Prompt" at the top of the page. Choose your card type (Basic, Cloze+, or Cloze++).

        

        

          

            
          

          
2. Copy the prompt and open any AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.). Paste the prompt, then paste your raw notes below it.

        

        

          

            
          

          
3. The AI outputs only the card text — already formatted correctly. Copy it and paste it straight into Text2Anki's notes box.

        

        

          

            
          

          
4. Download your .apkg and import into Anki. Done — from notes to cards in under a minute.

        

      

    


    
    

      
Customising card styles

      
Visit Card Styles to change background colours, text colours, font sizes, corner radius, and more for each card type. Save your favourite looks as named presets and select them per-download using the Card Theme dropdown on the main page.
